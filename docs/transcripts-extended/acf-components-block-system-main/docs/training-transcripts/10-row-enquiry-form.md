# 10. Row: Enquiry Form

**Summary:** Walkthrough of the Enquiry Form layout: written content, the contact form embed
field, and its card-only Grid & Display tab. **Example page:** Training: Enquiry Form
(`training-enquiry-form`, draft).

---

Enquiry Form is a small but important layout, it's how we drop a contact form into a Page
Builder page.

[ON SCREEN: open Training: Enquiry Form]

Content is a wysiwyg box, usually a short heading and your contact details, phone and email,
above the form itself. Then Contact form embed or shortcode, this is where the actual form goes,
paste in your shortcode, on this site that's a Contact Form 7 shortcode, similar to the one on the
training example.

A word of caution: that field only accepts the shortcode itself, this layout doesn't build the
form for you, the form has to already exist, created in Contact Form 7, before you can embed it
here. If you need a new form or a different one, that's a job for whoever manages the forms
plugin, not something you'd do inside this row.

Grid & Display only gives you Content Box on this layout, same as Call to Action, switch it on
for a card background. On the training page it's set to Card, Green background, which is what
gives that section its boxed, standout look.

Other Settings, as always: background, ID, padding.

Straightforward once you know it: write your intro, paste your shortcode, decide whether it sits
in a card, done.
