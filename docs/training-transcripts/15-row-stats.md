# 15. Row: Stats

**Summary:** Walkthrough of the Stats layout: the repeating stat, subtitle and content items,
alignment, optional buttons per item, and its full Grid & Display tab. **Example page:**
Training: Stats (`training-stats`, draft, four sample stats).

---

Stats, for leading with a number, four bedrooms, twenty-two units, a percentage, each with a
short line underneath.

[ON SCREEN: open Training: Stats]

One repeater: Stat is the headline figure, plain text so you're not limited to whole numbers,
then Subtitle, Content, and alignment. Each stat can carry its own Buttons too, optional.

Grid & Display gets the full set, here it's four columns dropping to one, seamless, on a Yellow
background.

Other Settings as always.

Keep it to a small number of punchy figures, past six or seven, Icon List or Columned Content
reads better.
