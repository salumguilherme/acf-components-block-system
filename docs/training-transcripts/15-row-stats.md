# 15. Row: Stats

**Summary:** Walkthrough of the Stats layout: the repeating stat, subtitle and content items,
alignment, optional buttons per item, and its full Grid & Display tab. **Example page:**
Training: Stats (`training-stats`, draft, four sample stats).

---

Stats is for the kind of row you'd use to lead with a number, four bedrooms, twenty-two units, a
percentage, that sort of thing, each one with a short line of supporting text underneath.

[ON SCREEN: open Training: Stats]

In Content, the Stats repeater, one row per stat. Stat is the headline figure itself, entered as
plain text so you're not limited to whole numbers, on the training page you'll see "24/7" and "3
km" alongside plain numbers. Subtitle is a short line under the figure, Content is a slightly
longer wysiwyg box below that, and Content Alignment sets how that item's text lines up, Left,
Center, or Right. Each stat can also carry its own Buttons, entirely optional, most stats won't
need one.

Grid & Display gets the full set, columns for desktop, tablet, mobile, alignment, and Content
Box. On the training page it's four columns on desktop, dropping to two on tablet and one on
mobile, seamless rather than cards, on a Yellow background.

Other Settings, as always: background, ID, padding.

A quick distinction worth knowing: this is for a small number of punchy figures, not a long list,
past six or seven items, Icon List or Columned Content will probably read better.
