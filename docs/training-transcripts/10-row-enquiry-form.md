# 10. Row: Enquiry Form

**Summary:** Walkthrough of the Enquiry Form layout: written content, the contact form embed
field, and its card-only Grid & Display tab. **Example page:** Training: Enquiry Form
(`training-enquiry-form`, draft).

---

Enquiry Form, how we drop a contact form into a Page Builder page.

[ON SCREEN: open Training: Enquiry Form]

Content is your intro, heading and contact details. Then the form embed field, paste your
shortcode in, on this site that's Contact Form 7.

One thing to flag: that field only takes the shortcode, the form itself has to already exist,
that's a job for whoever manages the forms plugin.

Grid & Display only gives you Content Box here, same as Call to Action, on the training page it's
Card, Green.

Other Settings as always: background, ID, padding.

Write your intro, paste your shortcode, decide on the card, done.
