# 07. Row: Call to Action

**Summary:** Walkthrough of the Call to Action layout: block layout, button alignment, content,
its buttons repeater, and its card-only Grid & Display tab. **Example page:** Training: Call to
Action (`training-cta`, draft, one row with a single download button).

---

Call to Action is your closing nudge, a short message and a button near the bottom of a page.

[ON SCREEN: open Training: Call to Action]

Block Layout picks Columns or Stacked, message and button side by side, or centred and stacked.
Button Alignment appears once you've added a button.

Content is a short box, this layout wants a line or heading, not a paragraph, then Buttons, same
repeater as before.

On the training page it's Columns, one Orange button with a download icon.

Grid & Display only gives Call to Action the Content Box half, switch it to Card for its own
background, here it's Green.

Other Settings as usual: background, ID, padding.

Simple layout doing a specific job, get the words and the button right.
