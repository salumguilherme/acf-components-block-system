# 13. Row: Image Gallery

**Summary:** Walkthrough of the Image Gallery layout: the images field, aspect ratio, its
columns-only Grid & Display tab, and what it becomes on the front end, a carousel with a
lightbox. **Example page:** Training: Image Gallery (`training-image-gallery`, draft, six sample
photos).

---

Image Gallery, a scrolling strip of photos with a lightbox.

[ON SCREEN: open Training: Image Gallery]

Images is a standard gallery field, order matters. Aspect Ratio is required, written as width to
height, the default's 1.42 to 1, every image gets cropped to whatever you set.

Grid & Display only gives you columns here, no alignment or card. On the training page it's three
columns dropping to one, six photos in as an example.

On the front end this becomes a swipeable carousel with a click-to-enlarge lightbox, built in,
nothing extra to configure.

Upload your photos, pick your ratio, set your columns, done.
