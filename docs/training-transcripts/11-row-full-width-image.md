# 11. Row: Full Width Image

**Summary:** Walkthrough of the Full Width Image layout: the background image, overlay and
overlay colour, content, and buttons, typically a page's hero section. **Example page:**
Training: Full Width Image (`training-full-width-image`, draft).

---

Full Width Image is almost always your hero, the big image at the top of a page with a heading
and a button sitting on top of it.

[ON SCREEN: open Training: Full Width Image]

Upload your Image first, this spans the whole width of the page behind everything else. Then
Overlay, Gradient or Solid, this is a tint that sits between the image and your text, it's what
keeps white heading text readable over a busy photo. Turn it off entirely if your image is
already dark enough, or if you'd rather the photo show through untouched. Overlay Colour is a
colour picker that supports transparency, so you're not limited to plain black, on the training
example it's a dark tint at about seventy percent opacity.

Below that, Content is your wysiwyg box for the heading and a short line underneath, and Buttons
is the same repeater as always, on this training page there's one button, "Enquire Now," jumping
down to the enquiry form with the chat icon before the text.

No Grid & Display tab on this one either, it's a single full-width block. Other Settings gives
you background, ID, and padding as usual, though on a hero like this you'd rarely touch the
background colour since the image is doing that job.

This is the layout that sets the tone for a page, get the image and the overlay balance right and
everything else follows.
